## DATA VALIDATION METHODOLOGY

### How Temperature Ranges Were Determined

1. **Primary Source**: Official FDA/EMA approved package inserts and prescribing information
2. **Secondary Source**: Manufacturer storage and handling guidelines
3. **Tertiary Source**: WHO technical reports and international pharmacopeias
4. **Validation**: Cross-referenced with USP monographs and AABB standards

### Humidity Requirements

- Standard pharmaceutical storage humidity: 30-60% RH (Relative Humidity)
- Based on: USP Chapter <1079> and ICH Q1A(R2) Stability Guidelines
- Critical for preventing degradation and maintaining product efficacy

### Quality Assurance

All data points have been:
- ✅ Verified against multiple authoritative sources
- ✅ Cross-referenced with regulatory guidelines
- ✅ Validated with current (2023-2024) manufacturer specifications
- ✅ Confirmed with pharmacopeial standards (USP, BP, EP)
